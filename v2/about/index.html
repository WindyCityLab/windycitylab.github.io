I’m Kevin.
I like making things work.

I’m an engineer, educator, and independent consultant based in Chicago. Windy City Lab is my consulting practice. When you hire WCL, you work with me.

I’m semi-retired and choose a small number of projects that interest me—particularly AI education and helping companies put AI to practical use.

A few chapters

Engineering, business, and teaching.

Different settings have taught me to look at the whole problem: the technology, the people, and the work that needs doing.

01

Avionics & software architecture

After studying computer science and digital electronics at Illinois Institute of Technology, I worked in embedded avionics at General Dynamics. At Rational Software, I moved from architecture consulting to leading the professional services organization worldwide.

02

Building a business

In 1999, I co-founded Copain Wine Cellars in Sonoma County and helped build Copain Custom Crush, a production-services business supporting more than 30 boutique wine producers. It gave me a firsthand education in operations, customers, and the work of running a company.

03

Products & technical leadership

My work has included mobile applications, cloud services, embedded hardware, connected devices, and CTO engagements. Through Deck5 Software and Windy City Lab, I’ve worked across the path from an idea to a working product.

04

Teaching by building

I developed iOS curriculum used in Chicago-area high schools, taught professional workshops, and now teach intensive technology courses through Northwestern University’s Center for Talent Development. My curriculum spans AI and computer engineering.
