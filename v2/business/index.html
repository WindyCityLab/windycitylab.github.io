How I can help

A practical path from question to use.

01

Find a useful starting point

Review a workflow with the people doing it. Identify the friction, the available data, and the places where AI could help. Agree on a focused first project and what would make it worthwhile.

02

Build & integrate

Develop a prototype or custom tool, connect it to the systems it needs, and test it against real examples. Decide which steps can be automated and where a person should review the result.

03

Help your team adopt it

Work through the new process together, document how it works, and help people learn when to trust the tool and when to question it. Refine it based on actual use.
