The teaching problem

Students need a way to connect AI concepts to software they can build, test, and explain.

The approach

A project-based sequence covering models, context, APIs, data, agents, and deployment. Students develop connected systems within a shared simulated world.

What it demonstrates

Curriculum that connects conceptual foundations to substantial engineering work, with projects that make understanding visible.

Turing Guild LMS

A learning-management system shaped by my own teaching.

The need

Keep the course structure, resources, labs, quizzes, and grading together in a system that fits the way I teach technical subjects.

What I built

A learning-management system with web and native interfaces for delivering curriculum and managing the work around a course.

Where AI fits

I use AI as an engineering collaborator to build and refine the software. This is an example of development with AI, rather than a claim that every LMS feature is powered by it.

Independent product · AI in the workflow

Scritorio

An AI editorial studio that keeps the author in charge.

Visit Scritorio

The need

Give authors useful editorial assistance across a book project while preserving their voice and control of the work.

What I built

A local-first environment for organizing writing, examining structure and continuity, and working with AI editorial perspectives.

Where AI fits

AI participates in editorial review. The author decides what to accept and how to revise. That relationship between assistance and judgment also shapes my consulting.
